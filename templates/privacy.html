Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. When you use the app, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, saving your expenses, and generating the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your expense data with third-party services. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting or database services) solely to run the application, and these providers do not have independent access to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
